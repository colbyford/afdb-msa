AFDB MSA

Paste a sequence. The closest entry in AlphaFold DB is found, its precomputed alignment is re-indexed onto your query's numbering, and you get an a3m back — paired across chains if you give more than one. No alignment is computed: AFDB already did it.

Runs entirely in your browser. An exact sequence resolves by checksum in about a second; anything else is looked up in a static index of 239.6M AlphaFold DB entries using a few dozen ranged reads (~50 KB), with EBI BLAST behind it as a fallback. No server, nothing uploaded anywhere else.
